## Goal

Deliver `/ops`: an `ops-footprint` panel driven only by `/v1/admin/status` that shows disk use, scoring state, failures, rejections and rescore progress for an authorised administrator, and nothing but a named "administrator only" state for anyone else.

## Contract

- `GET /v1/admin/status` is session-only and admin-only. Response: `{version, scoring:{scorer,checks,rescorable}, volume?:{total_bytes,free_bytes}, tenants:[{id,name,facts,compaction,footprint:{ledger_bytes,wal_bytes,archive_bytes,archive_files,blob_bytes,blobs,pending_bytes,pending,other_bytes}, screens:{verdicts:{...},scored_content,score_rows,rejections:[{stage,pattern,count,last}]|null,total_rejections}, failures:[{stage,pattern,reason,path,content_hash,at}], rescore:{running,total,done,failed,started,finished,error?}, error?}]}`. `started`/`finished`/`at` are Unix seconds; sizes are bytes; `error` on one tenant means only that tenant could not be read.
- **Only authorised status.** Render exactly this response. Do not infer admin from `/v1/me`, localStorage or a URL parameter; a `403` renders the frame's `unsupported` state with "administrator only" and no tenant values. A `401` is the shell's session gate. No other analytics/tenant request may be made to fill a gap; if a field is absent, say so. This panel is read-only: do not call `POST /v1/admin/rescore` or any other mutation; "rescore" means showing its recorded state.
- **Honest display.** Every number names its unit (bytes, facts, rows, rejections, seconds). Tenants are separate; never sum across tenants into one blended usage or disk figure. Show `screens.scored_content`/`score_rows`, verdicts, rejections by stage (at most the returned list), `total_rejections`; failures capped by the server (25) and labelled as recent; `compaction`; `footprint.pending`/`pending_bytes`; `volume` only when supplied; `rescore` running/total/done/failed/error with times. Unpriced/unscored are not `$0` or success.
- **States and basis.** Use the 9-01 frame vocabulary: `loading`, `ready`, `unsupported` (403 with "administrator only", or a server feature absent), `error` (message/code/retry), `cancelled`; no `empty` data invented. Name the basis (server status snapshot, not a historical series) and that it is point-in-time.
- **Accessibility.** A real table or definition list per tenant with scoped headers, captions, keyboard traversal and visible focus; no animation/transition; single column below 900 px.
